### My Journey as 8 years Basketball head.

## Basketball

It was May 4, 2019, around 10am, my mother and I were watching TV. There was nothing on the regular channel I used to watch. So here I am, just randomly flipping through the television channel, then I came across Sony Ten 1 HD and a basketball game was being played. And I decided why not. Let's watch my first ever basketball game. At that time, I had heard about the NBA but did not know any players except Jordan, obviously, and did not know any basketball rules at all. I think it was 3rd or 4th quater when I tuned into the channel. The first 30 minutes were the most confused I have ever been. It did not take me long to understand the 3-pointer, 2-pointer and free-throw foul. And other rules I was not able to grasp at all during the whole game. Rules like and-1, substitution and what constitutes a foul (I still don't know the rule in 2026).


Two teams were playing; One in Blue and the other one in Red & White. After 10 minutes, I quickly knew who was the best player in the Red & White Team. It was Damien Lillard. Although CJ McCollum was playing better, you just knew Lillard was the best player if you follow any kind of sport. But I couldn't say the same for the team in Blue. I genuinely thought Murry was their best player and Jokic was their 2nd best player. I continued to believe that until the 2021 season. The blue team was the Nuggets and the red and white team was Portland. While I was watching the game, I could not grasp how shooting 50% from the field is actually impressive. Just put the basketball in the net, how difficult that is. Later on, I realized how difficult it is to play basketball. So anyway, back to the game, the regular time concluded in overtime. And it went to 1st overtime, then 2nd overtime, then 3rd overtime and finally 4th overtime. When the game ended, I was so happy and so excited and also tired from watching the game. In the end, Portland wore and i wasn't so happy because I was supporting the Nuggets. Why not? Pick a team and start supporting them. The best thing about being an NBA watcher from another country is, you can just switch teams when your favorite team starts to become bad. That's what I did. When Portland won the series and faced the Warriors, I started to root for the Warriors because I found Steph's game so entertaining. His long range 3s were so fun to watch as a beginner at basketball. I had no idea GSW were the most hated team in the league by a wide margin. I did not know about Durant's situation and all the other stuff.

I watched the western conference finals between Portland vs Warriors. One of the talking points of that series is about battle between the two curry brothers. I did not watch any eastern series because the time zone was absolutely brutal when eastern team were playing.

So it was the finals, Raptors and Warriors in the finals. I had no idea about players like Kawhi, Siakam, FFV, Ibaka, etc. I only knew only handful of players from GSW who played in the WCF against Portland. I remember GSW pushing Durant through the injury and he did play but instantly getting injured in game 3 or game 4. The funniest part of that injury was the Raptor crowd were cheering when Durant got injured and i was like WTF. It was such a funny scenario. The same game or the next game, Klay gets injured and doesnot return. And I knew the series was over then and there.
I remember Steph shooting a deep 3 to tie the game and he missed and Kerr was saying 'It's okay' to Steph because they knew the game and the series was over. At the end, Raptors won in Game 6 where every player was playing specatular. I was so impressed with Pascal Siakam with how he played during the whole series but obviously, Kawhi was their best player who deservengly won the Finals MVP.
